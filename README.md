A top-down open-world driving game set in Sunset Bay, a coastal city
bathed in golden hour light. You play Mateo Vega, a getaway driver
caught between one last job and the whole city watching.

## The game

- Explore a living coastal city: pier, beachfront, downtown streets,
  and winding hillside roads, all under a warm sunset sky.
- 8 story missions that escalate from quiet pickups to full-speed
  escapes across Sunset Bay.
- Witness-based wanted system: get spotted committing a crime and
  the heat rises; break line of sight and lay low to cool off.
- Police pursuits that ramp up with your wanted level, from patrol
  cars shadowing you to roadblocks and aggressive chases.
- Dense traffic to weave through, dodge, or shove aside — every
  near-miss and collision feeds the chaos around you.
- Full HUD with speed, wanted stars, mission objectives, and cash,
  plus a live minimap tracking you, targets, and pursuing police.

## How to run

Built for desktop browsers. From the repo root:

```bash
node serve.ts
```

Then open the served address in your browser and drive.
